01 · The objective

Turn a bold idea
into a working strategy.

The challenge asks teams to improve autonomous behaviour in a shared simulation. Understand how agents perceive, decide, and act; change a strategy in agents.py; and show what changes for the colony.

Understand

Trace agent behaviour in Mesa.

Improve

Change one decision while keeping the world fixed.

Evaluate

Compare with the baseline using the same seeds.

Communicate

Explain the decision and its trade-offs.

02 · Metrics that matter

More than a lucky run.

Use these measures in every experiment. Final weighting and evaluation seeds will be announced before submissions open.

Primary outcome

Steps survived

How long did the colony remain operational? Higher is better.

Supporting measure

Resources delivered

How many resource units reached the base? Cargo still on a robot does not count.

Supporting measure

Distance travelled

How much movement did the fleet use? Interpret this together with survival and delivery.

Across scenarios

Robustness

Does the strategy work across seeds, rather than only once?

Recommended ranking principle

Rank primarily by average survival across shared scenarios. Use delivered resources and distance as supporting evidence or tie-breakers.

03 · Judging principles

We value results
we can trust.

Performance

Does the colony survive longer than baseline?

Generalisation

Does the improvement persist on new scenarios?

Design quality

Is the strategy understandable and extendable?

Evidence

Can others reproduce and assess the comparison?

Special recognition may reward a clear explanation or interesting failure analysis. The highest score is not automatically the most educational result.

04 · Suggested workflow

Five steps from idea
to mission report.

  1. RunRecord baseline
  2. ObserveFind weakness
  3. PredictState hypothesis
  4. ChangeEdit strategy
  5. CompareRepeat and explain

Begin with one controlled change. Once you understand its effect, combine improvements carefully and measure again.
